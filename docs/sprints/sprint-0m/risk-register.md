# Production multi-platform risk register

- **Register date:** 2026-08-08 (Asia/Riyadh)
- **Status:** **PROPOSED — owners and acceptance require approval**
- **Review cadence:** at every phase entry/exit, before each store submission, and immediately after a material provider/policy/architecture change

## Scale

- **P0:** credible risk of credential compromise, cross-user data exposure, unrecoverable data loss, or widespread integrity failure; blocks release.
- **P1:** credible risk of broken authentication, migration/restore failure, store rejection, major accessibility failure, or supported-client outage; blocks the affected phase/release unless explicitly reduced.
- **P2:** material delivery, maintainability, cost, or degraded-experience risk; needs an owner and tracked mitigation.
- **P3:** bounded inconvenience or optimization risk; monitor and schedule deliberately.

Likelihood (`L/M/H`) is the planning estimate before mitigation. Impact is the worst credible effect. **INFERRED** ratings must be updated with executed evidence.

## Review procedure

At each review:

1. **PROPOSED** — confirm the evidence label and link to executed proof.
2. **PROPOSED** — update likelihood/impact and record residual risk after controls.
3. **PROPOSED** — name one accountable owner and due phase/date.
4. **PROPOSED** — convert P0/P1 mitigation into an executable release gate.
5. **PROPOSED** — record accepted residual risk with owner/date/reason; silence is not acceptance.
6. **PROPOSED** — close a risk only when the trigger is impossible or evidence proves the control; implemented code alone is not closure.
